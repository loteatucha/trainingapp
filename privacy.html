Privacy Policy

Training App · Last updated 8 June 2026

This is a personal training-tracking app built and used by a single individual (the developer). It is not a commercial product and has no other users. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Who this applies to

The app is operated by Cristobal De Atucha for personal use. The only person whose data is accessed is the developer (the account owner who authorizes the connections).

Data the app accesses

When you connect your accounts, the app may read the following, strictly to display it back to you inside the app:

  • WHOOP — recovery score, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, and daily strain/cycle data, via the WHOOP API using scopes such as read:recovery, read:sleep, read:cycles, read:workout, and read:profile.
  • Strava — your activities (runs, swims, weight training) including distance, duration, pace, heart rate, and effort metrics.
  • Profile basics — name and measurement preference, used only for display.

How the data is used

  • To show your training and recovery metrics inside this app.
  • That is the only purpose. The data is not sold, advertised against, or shared with any third party.

How the data is stored

  • Workout logs you enter are stored locally in your own browser on your device.
  • Synced WHOOP and Strava data is stored in the app's own private storage (the account owner's device and the account owner's private cloud storage).
  • No data is stored on shared or public infrastructure.

Revoking access

You can disconnect at any time:

  • WHOOP — revoke under your WHOOP account's connected-apps settings.
  • Strava — revoke under Strava → Settings → My Apps.

Revoking immediately stops any further access to your data.

Data retention & deletion

Because the app is single-user and local, you can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the app's storage on your device. To request deletion, contact the address below.
